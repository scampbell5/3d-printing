## Modifications for this remix

Created a separate sliding sleeve cover; changed the bottom to shallow chamfered Gridfinity-style feet; reconstructed the holder into a single connected watertight mesh; removed external projecting rails to reduce required supports; added small detent pockets and catches. The revised design has not yet had its complete physical fit validated.
