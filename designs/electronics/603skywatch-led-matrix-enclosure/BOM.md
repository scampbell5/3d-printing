# Suggested enclosure hardware

- 1 × 64×32 P4 LED panel
- 1 × Huidu HD-WF2 controller
- 2 × WAGO 221-415 5-port lever connectors
- 1 × panel-mount 5V DC barrel jack matching the external supply
- 4 × panel mounting screws matching the panel's brass inserts
- 2 × M3 plastic-forming screws for the hidden cassette safety locks (optional but recommended)
- 2 × M3 heat-set inserts + M3 screws for the removable feet
- Small adhesive rubber pads for the feet
- PETG filament for the final enclosure

The enclosure is designed for regulated 5V DC only. Keep AC mains outside the printed case in the external power brick.
